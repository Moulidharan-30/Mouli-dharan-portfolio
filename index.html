Hey! I'm Mouli — a UI/UX designer and front-end developer based in India. I believe great products are born at the intersection of beautiful design, solid engineering, and deep domain understanding.

What sets me apart is where I come from. Before transitioning into design, I spent 1.5 years in the healthcare sector at Cognizant — which gave me something most designers don't have: real-world insight into high-stakes user environments, complex workflows, and the cost of getting UX wrong. That experience now shapes how I approach every design problem, whether it's health tech or beyond. I craft pixel-perfect interfaces and seamless user journeys, and I bring the technical depth to see them through from wireframe to working product.
